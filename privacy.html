ClipFarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Overview

ClipFarm is a desktop application that helps users create, review, and publish short-form video content. This policy explains how ClipFarm handles information when users connect supported social media accounts.

Information We Access

When a user connects a supported social media account, ClipFarm may receive authorization tokens and basic account information required to provide the requested integration. ClipFarm only requests permissions needed for the features the user chooses to use.

Video Content

Videos selected by the user may be uploaded to connected social media platforms when the user chooses to publish them. ClipFarm does not claim ownership of user content.

Storage

ClipFarm is designed as a desktop application. Application files, generated clips, and locally stored authorization data may remain on the user's own computer unless a connected platform requires data to be transmitted for a requested action.

Third-Party Services

ClipFarm may connect to third-party platforms such as TikTok and YouTube. Use of those services is also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

User Control

Users may stop using ClipFarm or disconnect authorized social media access at any time through the relevant platform's account settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the ClipFarm developer through the contact information associated with the application's official developer account.
